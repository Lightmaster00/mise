# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- New Light section: soft shadows of leaves, a window or blinds cast across the whole scene, with strength, angle and a shuffle button.
- Turned phones and tablets show a rounded metal side: shadowed at the back, a bright line in the middle.
- Thinner phone borders, closer to current phones.
- Templates with colored studio walls and cast light.

## 1.1.0

- Devices look real: polished metal frames lit from the top left, rim highlights, glass glare, camera lenses, ridged watch crown, laptop hinge and base.
- Turned devices show their thickness, and every item now sits on a two-layer studio shadow (soft ambient plus a tight contact shadow).
- New Studio background: a seamless wall curving into the floor, lit from above, with a light vignette and grain.
- Paper, cards, posters and books get fibre grain, light falloff and a believable page block.
- A calm sample app (mobile or dashboard) replaces the old gradient placeholder until you drop a design.
- Templates use quieter, photographic backdrops.
- New icon: a phone floating in a cream studio.

## 1.0.0

First release: vector devices and print items, 3D perspective, compositions, backgrounds, photo scenes, website capture, 31 templates, PNG/JPEG/HEIC export up to 4x, batch export, projects with undo.
